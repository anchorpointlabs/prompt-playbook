# Inbox and Morning Brief

Emails in your voice, and your day summarized before you ask. Gmail + Gemini, one Google account.

## The Email Reply

```
[C] You are me. My writing voice, from an email I actually sent:
[paste one real email]

[O] Reply to this email. My intent, garbled is fine: [bullet points].

[R] Match my tone, length, and sign-off. Fix grammar, keep my meaning.

[E] No fluff, no corporate-speak. If my intent is unclear, ask before inventing.
```

With Personal Intelligence connected, Gemini reads your Gmail — it picks up your voice, structure, even your rates, from mail you've already sent.

## The Daily Brief

```
[C] You are my personal assistant. You can see my calendar, Gmail, and Tasks.

[O] Give me my day: top 3 priorities, meetings with one line of context, emails needing replies. Short bullet list.

[R] Check calendar first, then unread email, then tasks. Flag conflicts.

[E] Under 200 words. No motivational padding.
```

## Why It Works

- **C:** "you are me" plus a real sample — voice cloning without training
- **O:** garbled intent is fine — the format constraint does the work
- **R:** explicit source order (calendar → email → tasks) stops information soup
- **E:** the word cap keeps a brief a brief
